# Getting Started with TheAndb AI Assistant

TheAndb AI Assistant is your companion for database schema analysis, performance optimization, and general guidance.

## 1. Accessing the AI Assistant
You can open the AI Assistant by clicking the **Sparkles** icon on the right sidebar. It will automatically detect the context of what you are currently viewing (e.g., a specific table or DDL).

## 2. AI Personas
You can change how the AI interacts with you in **Settings > AI Assistant**:
- **Expert DBA 👴**: Detailed technical reviews, performance-focused.
- **Secretary 👩‍💼**: Concise summaries and organizational help.
- **Co-worker 🤝**: Casual and collaborative problem-solving.
- **Playful 🎮**: Fun, humorous, and witty interaction.

## 3. Schema Review
When you view a table's DDL, you can trigger an **AI Review** to:
- Detect potential performance bottlenecks.
- Check for naming convention consistency.
- Suggest missing indexes or constraints.
- Validate safety of destructive operations.

## 4. Privacy & Safety
The AI assistant only analyzes the schema (DDL) and metadata you provide. It never accesses your actual row data unless explicitly shared.
